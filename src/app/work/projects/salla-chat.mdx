## Overview

[Salla Chat](https://apps.salla.sa/en/app/313987978) brings customer conversations from multiple social channels into a single interface in the store dashboard—so support is no longer scattered across apps. I worked on this product while at [Salla](https://salla.com/).

## Highlights

- **Omnichannel inbox**: Handle customer messages from one place, inside Salla.
- **Team management**: Add teammates, define roles, and automate chat assignment for smoother service.
- **Automated marketing on WhatsApp**: Abandoned-cart reminders, order updates, and review requests sent automatically.

## Outcome

Merchants get a unified support surface with workflows that scale from solo founders to larger teams—without leaving the Salla ecosystem.
