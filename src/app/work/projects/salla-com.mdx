## Overview

I contributed to [salla.com](https://salla.com/) during my time at **Salla E-Commerce Platform**, helping ship and iterate on the main marketing site that introduces the platform, products, and ecosystem to merchants.

## Focus areas

- **Product storytelling**: Clear positioning for merchants evaluating Salla.
- **Performance and UX**: Fast, responsive pages aligned with the brand.
- **Cross-team delivery**: Working with product, design, and engineering in a shipping cadence that matches the rest of the platform.

## Outcome

A credible, up-to-date front door for the company that supports acquisition and trust for tens of thousands of merchants.
