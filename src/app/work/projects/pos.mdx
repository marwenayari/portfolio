## Overview

[Salla Point](https://apps.salla.sa/en/app/1268123532) makes it easy for merchant teams to create orders directly in front of the customer, with real-time updates to inventory and orders, plus clear reports to track performance. I contributed to this product as part of my work at [Salla](https://salla.com/).

## Highlights

- **Create and manage orders**: Draft orders, instant discounts (percentage or fixed), custom line items not in the catalog, and order notes.
- **Flexible payments**: Card NFC SoftPOS in the app, cash, and payment links or QR codes so customers can pay with the store’s enabled methods (for example Apple Pay, Tabby, and Tamara).
- **Real-time sync**: Sales update inventory automatically and link each order to the merchant’s Salla store.

## Outcome

A focused POS experience that keeps storefront, inventory, and payments aligned—reducing manual steps for staff and giving merchants a clearer view of in-person sales.
