## FAQ

**Is the app free?** Yes, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

**Does it work offline?** Content you downloaded works offline. Streaming needs a connection.

**Does it keep playing when the screen is locked?** Yes.

**How does the sleep timer work?** You pick a duration, and the app gradually lowers the volume and then stops.

**What about my data?** We collect none. See the [Privacy Policy](/mazeej/en/privacy).
