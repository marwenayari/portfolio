# Privacy Policy — Mazeej

Last updated: 22 November 2026

**In short: Mazeej does not collect any data about you.**

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- We do not collect your name, email, location or device identifiers.
- We do not sell or share data with anyone.

## What stays on your device

Your settings (reciter, sound levels, sleep timer) and the recitations you download for offline listening. These stay on your device and are never sent to us.

## Network access

When you stream or download a recitation, the app connects to mp3quran.net servers to fetch the audio file. Like any web server, it may see the IP address and technical details of the request. We do not receive this information. See that provider's own policy for details. Downloaded content then plays without a connection.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
