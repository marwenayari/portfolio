# Mazeej — Recitation & Nature Sounds

Pick your reciter, add rain, sea or wind, and control the level of each sound.

**Mazeej** is an iOS app that gently blends Quran recitation with nature sounds. It is free, has no ads, and needs no account.

- Choose a reciter and surah, then blend with nature sounds
- Independent volume control for each sound
- Sleep timer that fades the audio out gradually
- Download content for offline listening
- Keeps playing on the lock screen and in the background
