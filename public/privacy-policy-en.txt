Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

1. Introduction

At "Tazakka", we value your privacy. This application is designed to be simple, secure, and focused solely on helping you calculate your Zakat easily and accurately, without collecting or sharing your personal data.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data from users.

- No name or email address is collected
- No location tracking is performed
- No financial or sensitive information is collected
- No user data is stored on external servers

All data you enter in the app (such as wallets and amounts) remains stored locally on your device only.

3. Advertising

- The app does not contain any advertisements
- No advertising tracking tools are used

4. In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases as a way to support the app.

- These purchases are completely optional
- They do not unlock any additional features
- Payments are processed through Apple App Store
- We do not access or store any payment information

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use limited third-party services such as:

- RevenueCat for managing in-app purchases

These services may process minimal technical data (such as subscription status) but do not share personal user data with us.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data:

- Your data remains on your device
- No data is transmitted to external servers

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
